About WEVON

Built to represent your brand with quality.

WEVON Apparel is a Sri Lankan apparel manufacturer focused on high-quality, customized garment solutions for businesses, institutions, teams and organizations. We combine skilled craftsmanship, modern production techniques and quality-focused processes to create garments designed to be durable, comfortable and professionally finished.

From small custom requirements to bulk orders, our goal is simple: deliver apparel that represents your brand with care, precision and pride.

Our Production Process

From concept to finished garment.

A clear, quality-focused workflow from your initial requirement through final delivery.

1

Requirement & Design

We understand your requirements, branding and intended use.

2

Fabric Selection

Materials are selected based on comfort, durability and performance.

3

Pattern & Sampling

Patterns and samples are prepared for fit, style and approval.

4

Cutting

Fabric is cut accurately to maintain consistency and reduce waste.

5

Stitching

Experienced operators assemble garments with attention to detail.

6

Embroidery & Printing

Branding and customization are applied for a professional finish.

7

Quality & Finishing

Items are checked, finished, ironed and prepared for packing.

8

Packing & Delivery

Orders are packed securely and prepared for timely delivery.
